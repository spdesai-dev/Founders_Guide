# Founder's Field Guide

> **How to get your first 100 customers**  
> A step-by-step guide for people building software products. Fifteen steps, in order, without requiring an ad budget to start.

## Overview

This guide breaks down founder-led growth into actionable steps:
- **Part 1 · Get clear**: Pick one customer, talk to 20 of them, and get your basics ready.
- **Part 2 · First customers**: Start with warm contacts, sell it yourself, and reach out one-by-one.
- **Part 3 · Show up**: Join communities, attend events, and build content.
- **Part 4 · Get a spike**: Launch platforms and lifetime deals.
- **Part 5 · Scale up**: Cold email, referrals, and strategic partners.
- **Part 6 · Measure**: Track key metrics and follow the weekly checklist.
